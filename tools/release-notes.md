**Install**

- [Apps2Samsung](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung): add `github.com/genesai/retroarch-tizen` as your own source, or install `RetroArch.wgt` below as your own `.wgt`

Cores are downloaded from https://genesai.github.io/retroarch-tizen/ on first start.
Requires a TV with Tizen 6.5+ (2022 or newer). Details in the README.
